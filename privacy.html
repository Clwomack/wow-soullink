WoW SoulLink privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

  • What it accesses. WoW SoulLink requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files it creates, kept in a "WoW SoulLink" folder in your own Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in your Drive.
  • What it stores. Copies of your World of Warcraft Interface\AddOns and WTF\Account folders, plus a small sync record. Your Google account email is read once to show which account is connected.
  • Where data goes. Data moves only between your PCs and your Google Drive. There is no WoW SoulLink server; nothing is sent to the developer or any third party, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in. Your Google sign-in token is stored on your PC, encrypted to your Windows account.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstall WoW SoulLink from Windows Settings, delete the "WoW SoulLink" folder from Google Drive, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

WoW SoulLink's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for WoW SoulLink.
